## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small concept-mark from an experimental notebook, not a finished machine and not a technology infographic. Reduce the subject to its structural idea: a hand-drawn ink contour, a few geometric nodes, halftone or stipple, and at most a little muted blue, green or theme color on warm off-white paper. Keep the mark small in a large field of editorial whitespace. Fragments of type may read as a specimen number, observation or short note, set small and slightly imperfect. Reject a complete scene, cyberpunk styling, smooth 3D rendering, piled diagrams and cartoon illustration.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
